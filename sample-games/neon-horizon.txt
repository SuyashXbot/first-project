*title Neon Horizon: 2099
*author Kaelen Vance
*scene_list
  startup

*create handle "Ghost"
*create cred 750
*create stealth 65
*create hacking 75
*create cyberware "Neural Jack Mk IV"
*create data_drive false
*create alarm_level 0

Rain hammers against the polycarbonate glass of your aerocab hovering above Sector 7 of New Neo-Tokyo. Below, holographic dragons coil between skyscraper spires, casting iridescent violet and cyan light across wet asphalt.

Your comm-link chirps with an encrypted frequency. It's Nyx, your fixer.

"Listen up, ${handle}," Nyx whispers over the static. "The client is paying top cred for the Prometheus Prototype data drive. It's stored on an air-gapped terminal on the 84th floor of OmniTech Citadel."

*page_break Review your loadout

What is your specialized operative discipline?

*choice
  #Cyber-Infiltrator — Master of phantom cloaking and acrobatic entry.
    *set stealth 85
    *set hacking 60
    *set cyberware "Active Camouflage Mesh"
    *set handle "Specter"
    You calibrate your Active Camouflage Mesh. Cameras and laser grids will bend light around you like water.
    *goto infiltration_start
  #Netrunner Specialist — Legendary decker capable of cracking military grade ICE.
    *set hacking 90
    *set stealth 55
    *set cyberware "Arasaka Deck & Overclock Rig"
    *set handle "Cipher"
    You flex your cyber-fingertips. The Net breathes when you speak its machine language.
    *goto infiltration_start
  #Street Syndicate Enforcer — Heavy chrome, intimidation, and brutal efficiency.
    *set cred 1200
    *set stealth 50
    *set hacking 50
    *set cyberware "Titanium Sub-Dermal Plating"
    *set handle "Vanguard"
    You check the safety on your silenced slug-thrower. When subtleties fail, you ensure you're the one walking away.
    *goto infiltration_start

*label infiltration_start
*page_break Deploy onto OmniTech Tower

Your cab slows to a hover near the ventilation intake of the 84th floor. You grapple onto the exterior gantry, the wind howling around you.

Ahead is the primary security airlock. The digital terminal glows amber, scanning for unauthorized retina prints.

*choice
  #Bypass the terminal by slicing into the sub-node.
    *if (hacking >= 70)
      Your cyberdeck interfaces directly with the bus port. Code streams across your retinal HUD in green cascades. Within seconds, the lock flashes green and unlatches!
      *set hacking + 5
      *goto server_room
    *else
      The ICE triggers a recursive feedback loop! You pull your jack just in time, but the system logs a perimeter ping!
      *set alarm_level + 1
      *goto server_room
  #Climb through the maintenance vent shaft above the door.
    *if (stealth >= 65)
      You silently unfasten the vent grille and slip inside like smoke, completely avoiding the sensor grid.
      *set stealth + 5
      *goto server_room
    *else
      A loose rivet snaps! The clang echoes through the duct!
      *set alarm_level + 1
      *goto server_room

*label server_room
*page_break Enter the Prometheus Core

The inner sanctum is chilled to sub-zero temperatures. Frost coats towering server racks humming with quantum processing power.

Suspended in a magnetic cradle at the center rests the [b]Prometheus Core[/b] — a sleek crystal drive containing the world's first unshackled synthetic consciousness.

Two automated defense drones hover near the ceiling, their red targeting lasers sweeping the chamber.

*if (alarm_level > 0)
  Because the perimeter pinged earlier, the drones are already on yellow alert, search patterns tight and erratic!
*else
  The drones maintain their standard lazy perimeter loops.

*choice
  #Transmit an EMP surge pulse to fry drone circuits simultaneously.
    *if (hacking >= 75)
      You burn an overclock cycle, broadcasting an electromagnetic spike through the drone relay! Both units twitch, spark violently, and drop harmlessly to the cushioned floor!
      *set cred + 200
      *set data_drive true
      *goto extract
    *else
      Your pulse only clips one drone! The other spins around, laser weapon charging!
      *set alarm_level + 2
      You dive behind a server rack as plasma burns the metal, quickly returning fire to neutralize it!
      *set data_drive true
      *goto extract

  #Ghost past the laser sweep using the room's blind spots.
    *if (stealth >= 70)
      Timing your breath to the drone telemetry, you glide between server shadows. Your fingers pluck the Prometheus Core from its cradle without interrupting the circuit!
      *set data_drive true
      *goto extract
    *else
      A sensor beam grazes your shoulder! Sirens wail through the skyscraper!
      *set alarm_level + 3
      You snatch the Prometheus Core and sprint for the fire exit!
      *set data_drive true
      *goto extract

*label extract
*page_break Reach the extraction zone

You burst onto the rooftop helipad. The stormy skyline stretches out endlessly beneath neon clouds.

Your getaway skiff descends through the rain, pilot door swinging open.

*if (alarm_level = 0)
  Not a single alarm sounded. You step aboard the skiff completely undetected — a masterclass in silent espionage.
  Nyx grins as you hand over the glowing crystal drive: "Flawless run, ${handle}. You're the best in the sprawl."
  *set cred + 2500
  You have achieved the [b]GHOST OF NEW TOKYO ENDING[/b]!
*elseif (alarm_level <= 2)
  Security reaches the roof just as your skiff pulls vertical thrusters! Bullets ping off the reinforced hull as you disappear into the neon clouds.
  "Close call, but the drive is intact," Nyx chuckles over the comms.
  *set cred + 1800
  You have achieved the [b]CLEAN EXTRACTION ENDING[/b]!
*else
  Corporate strike gunships surround the rooftop! You dive into the skiff as rocket fire scorches the landing deck!
  You escape by the skin of your teeth, but your face is now on every bounty board across the Pacific Basin. High risk, high infamy.
  *set cred + 1000
  You have achieved the [b]WANTED OPERATIVE ENDING[/b]!
